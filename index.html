About CyberSafe Nigeria

CyberSafe Nigeria is an educational website created to teach people how to identify and prevent online fraud.

Common Online Frauds

  • Phishing Scams
  • Fake Online Stores
  • Social Media Scams
  • Identity Theft
  • Investment Scams

Cyber Safety Tips

  • Never share your passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Keep your devices updated.
  • Always verify websites before making payments.

Can You Spot The Scam?

Your bank sends this message:

"Your account will be blocked in 5 minutes. Click this link immediately and enter your password."
